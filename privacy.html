Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Qentro BizOps (“the app”), an internal business operations tool made by Qentro AI Consulting (Xiaofang Yang), handles information, including data from Google accounts.

In short: the app runs entirely in your browser. It has no server. (The one exception is a sign-in log entry — see 3b.) Your data is stored on your device and in your own Google Drive and Google Calendar. Nothing is sent to Qentro AI Consulting or to any third party, and nothing is used for advertising, analytics or sale.

1. Who we are

Qentro BizOps is operated by Qentro AI Consulting (Xiaofang Yang) as an internal tool for managing business contacts, events, follow-ups, tasks and meeting notes. Contact: contact@qentrotech.com.

2. Google sign-in and the permissions we request

You can use the app without signing in (data then stays only in that browser). If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests:

  • Basic profile (openid, email, profile) — to show which account is connected and to suggest it again when you reconnect.
  • Google Drive — drive.file — access only to files the app itself creates or that you open with it. The app stores its data file, meeting transcripts (Markdown) and Excel backups in a folder named “Qentro BizOps” (formerly “Qentro Relationships” — the same folder, renamed) in your own Drive. It cannot see or change your other Drive files.
  • Google Calendar — calendar.events (optional, asked for only when you turn on calendar sync) — to create and update calendar events for your meetings and follow-ups when you turn that on. The app only changes events it created; you can turn calendar sync off in Settings at any time.

3. How the app uses Google Calendar data

  • Calendar access is optional and requested separately. Signing in never asks for it; the app asks only when you turn on calendar sync (Settings → Connect Google Calendar).
  • The app creates events on your primary calendar for meetings you schedule in the app and for dated follow-ups and tasks, updates them when you change the item, and deletes them when you complete or remove the item or turn that calendar option off.
  • Each event it creates is tagged privately. The app looks up only those tagged events. It does not read, change or delete any other event on your calendar, invites nobody and sends no emails.
  • The Calendar data the app handles (the titles, times, locations and descriptions of its own events) comes from what you entered in the app. It stays between your browser and your Google Calendar and is never sent to Qentro AI Consulting or anyone else.
  • To stop: turn calendar sync off (the app removes its events on the next sync), or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events you keep are yours to delete in Google Calendar at any time.

3a. What data the app handles

  • Information you enter: contacts, companies, events, interactions, tasks, time and mileage entries, projects, pipeline and financial figures, and notes.
  • Meeting transcripts: when you choose to record, speech is converted to text only by your browser’s speech recognition; no audio is recorded or stored. Before the first recording of a meeting the app asks you to confirm that attendees were told the meeting is transcribed. Depending on your browser and device, speech recognition may be processed by the browser or operating-system vendor (for example Apple, Google or Microsoft) under their own terms.
  • Your Google profile name, email and picture (if signed in).

3b. Sign-in log and access control

  • The app is private. Only Google accounts on the owner’s list may use it. Any other account is signed out right away, its access token is revoked, and the app never touches its Drive or Calendar.
  • Every sign-in attempt, allowed or blocked, is recorded in a Google Sheet in the owner’s own Google Drive (“Qentro BizOps Sign-in Log”). Each row holds the email address, name, time, result, device and browser type and the app version. The record is sent directly from your browser to a Google Apps Script owned by Qentro AI Consulting; it is used only to see who tried to access the app and is not shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • To have your sign-in record deleted, email contact@qentrotech.com.

4. Where data is stored

  • On your device: a local cache in the browser’s IndexedDB storage, so the app works offline. Settings → Forget this device (or signing out on a shared device) removes it.
  • In your Google account: the “Qentro BizOps” Drive folder and, if enabled, events on your Google Calendar.
  • Not on our servers: the app has no backend. Qentro AI Consulting does not receive, store or have access to your data.

5. Access tokens

The Google access token is kept in memory only — never written to storage — and is revoked when you sign out. You can also remove the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone. The only information Qentro AI Consulting receives is the sign-in log entry described in section 3b. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party scripts other than Google’s own sign-in library. Data leaves your device only to go to your own Google account through Google’s APIs, or when you yourself export, download or share a file.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use

Qentro BizOps’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features of the app described above; it is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you; it is not used for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalised or interest-based advertising; it is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes; and no human reads it unless you give explicit consent for specific messages or files, it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, to comply with applicable law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

8. Retention, deletion and erasure

  • You can delete any record in the app; deletions sync to your Drive data file and remove the matching calendar event the app created.
  • Data is kept only as long as you keep it: there is no copy on our side, so deleting it on your device, in your Drive folder and in your calendar removes it completely.
  • Per-person erasure deletes a contact together with their linked meeting transcripts, including the transcript files in Drive and older file revisions where Google allows it.
  • Forget this device removes the local copy; deleting the “Qentro BizOps” folder in Drive removes the synced copy; you control both entirely.
  • Files you exported or emailed are outside the app — delete those yourself and ask anyone you shared them with to do the same.

9. Security

The app is served over HTTPS with a strict Content-Security-Policy, refuses to run inside frames, and uses the narrowest Google permissions that support its features.

10. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and material changes will be noted in the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: contact@qentrotech.com.
